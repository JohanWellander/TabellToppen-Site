Help, subscriptions and account questions

TabellToppen is a leaderboard app for friends: create a tournament, add sports and players, log matches and follow the standings. Share a tournament with an invite code so everyone can log results.

FAQ

What does TabellToppen Pro include?

Pro is an auto-renewing yearly subscription that removes the free-plan limits (tournaments, sports and players) and unlocks Skill & HCP boards and random/fair team tools.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID, open Settings → TabellToppen Pro → Restore purchases in the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → TabellToppen and tap Cancel Subscription, or tap Manage subscription in the app’s Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next year. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your login, your profile and all shared tournaments you created (for all members), and removes you from tournaments you joined. It does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel that separately as described above. You can also email us to request deletion.

How do I remove data stored on my phone?

Use Settings → Clear all data on this phone, or uninstall the app.
